English profile

Nikerson Guillaume is an entrepreneur, qualified IT technician, and digital solutions designer. He founded Brindou’s Event Planners in 2020 and develops websites and applications for businesses, organizations, clients, and community initiatives.

Projects include the Brindou’s website and management suite, EDSVI Connect, Yallbe Marketplace (client project), and an in-development food delivery application for Haiti through True Patner Service, where he is a member and app developer.

Education: Computer technology diploma, Canado-Technique (2007–2010); administrative sciences studies at École de Droit et des Sciences Économiques des Gonaïves (2016–2019), no degree awarded.
